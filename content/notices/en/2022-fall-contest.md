#### Outline

The 2022 KAIST 12th ICPC Mock Competition will be held on October 3rd!

Participants in this competition will solve about 13 algorithm problems for 5 hours with a team of 3 members.

The competition will take place on-site on Monday, October 3rd, with the [Baekjoon Online Judge](https://acmicpc.net) platform. An online open contest (mirror) will be held after the competition ends.

#### Sponsorship

This contest is sponsored by [Devsisters](https://www.devsisters.com/), [Naver D2](https://d2.naver.com), [Startlink](http://startlink.io), [Moloco](https://www.moloco.com/), and [Nexon](https://www.nexon.com/).

Prizes worth 2,500,000 KRW in total will be given.

* 1st place: Amazon eGift $250 x 3
* 2nd place: Amazon eGift $150 x 3
* 3rd place: Amazon eGift $75 x 3
* Special prize (3 teams): awarded with criteria unrelated to rank
* Participation prize

#### Rules

* Only KAIST students, including graduate students, exchange students, students on leave, etc., can participate.
* You can only participate in a team of three.
* Each team must use exactly one computer (laptop). No websites or internet use are allowed during the contest except for official language reference sites and Baekjoon Online Judge.
* All team members must arrive at N1 room 102 by 11:30 am.
* The available languages are limited to C, C++, Java, Python, and Kotlin, using only the versions supported by BOJ.
* Ranking follows the [ICPC Regional Rules](https://icpc.baylor.edu/regionals/rules).
* Anything not specified here will be decided at the organizers' discretion.
