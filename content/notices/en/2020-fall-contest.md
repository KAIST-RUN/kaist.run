#### Outline

The 2020 KAIST 10th ICPC Mock Competition will be held on October 9th!

Participants in this competition will solve about 12 algorithm problems for 5 hours with a team of 3 members.

The competition will take place online, with the [Baekjoon Online Judge](https://acmicpc.net) platform. An online open contest (mirror) will be held simultaneously.

#### Sponsorship

This contest is sponsored by [Devsisters](https://www.devsisters.com/), [Naver D2](https://d2.naver.com), [Jane Street](https://www.janestreet.com/), [Startlink](http://startlink.io), and [Moloco](https://www.molocoads.com/).

#### Schedule

* Registration closes on October 7th (Wed) at 23:59.

#### Rules

* Only KAIST students, including graduate students, exchange students, etc., can participate.
* You can only participate in a team of three.
* Each team member may use their own computer simultaneously, and internet searches and pre-written code are allowed.
* All team members must join the ZOOM session by 11:00 am.
* The available languages are limited to C, C++, Java, Python, and Kotlin, using only the versions supported by BOJ.
* Ranking follows the [ICPC Regional Rules](https://icpc.baylor.edu/regionals/rules).
* Anything not specified here will be decided at the organizers' discretion.
