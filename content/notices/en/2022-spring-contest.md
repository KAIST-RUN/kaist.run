#### Outline

The 2022 KAIST RUN Spring Contest will be held on May 5th!

The competition will take place starting at 1:00 pm on Thursday, May 5th, in KAIST N1 room 102.

#### Sponsorship

This contest is sponsored by [Devsisters](https://www.devsisters.com/), [Naver D2](https://d2.naver.com), [Moloco](https://www.moloco.com/ko/), and [Startlink](http://startlink.io).

#### Schedule

* The competition will be held on May 5th (Thu).
* Participants must arrive at N1 room 102 by 12:30 pm.

```
Competition day schedule
12:30-13:00 | Competition guidance and environment setting
13:00-17:00 | Competition
17:00-17:20 | Seminar from sponsors (TBD)
17:20-      | Awards ceremony
```

#### Rules

* Only KAIST students, including graduate students, exchange students, etc., can participate.
* Each participant must bring their own laptop, laptop charger, and writing utensils.
* No internet or other reference materials may be consulted during the contest.
