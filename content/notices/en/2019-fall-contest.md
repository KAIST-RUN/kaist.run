#### Outline

The 2019 KAIST 9th ICPC Mock Competition will be held on October 3rd!

Participants in this competition will solve about 12 algorithm problems for 5 hours with a team of 3 members.

The competition will take place on Thursday, October 3rd, at N1 room 102, with the [Baekjoon Online Judge](https://acmicpc.net) platform. An online open contest (mirror) will be held simultaneously.

#### Sponsorship

This contest is sponsored by [Devsisters](https://www.devsisters.com/), [Naver D2](https://d2.naver.com), [Jane Street](https://www.janestreet.com/), and [Startlink](http://startlink.io).

#### Schedule

* Registration closes on October 1st (Tue) at 23:59.

The following prizes, worth 2,500,000 KRW in total, are sponsored by [Devsisters](https://www.devsisters.com/).

* 1st place: Prize worth 1,000k KRW
* 2nd place: Prize worth 700k KRW

Every other team will receive a participation prize.

#### Rules

* Only KAIST students, including graduate students, exchange students, etc., can participate.
* You can only participate in a team of three.
* Since no laptop will be provided, you must bring your own.
* At most one electronic device (laptop) may be used during the contest.
* At least one team member must arrive by 1:30 pm; all team members must arrive by 2:00 pm.
* The available languages are limited to C, C++, Java, Python, and Kotlin, using only the versions supported by BOJ.
* Internet access is prohibited during the contest except for submitting solutions and accessing language reference sites.
* Each team may bring up to 25 pages of printed A4-sized reference materials.
* Copying pre-written code already stored on the computer is considered cheating.
* Ranking follows the [ICPC Regional Rules](https://icpc.baylor.edu/regionals/rules).
* Anything not specified here will be decided at the organizers' discretion.
