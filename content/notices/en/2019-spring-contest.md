#### Outline

The 2019 KAIST RUN Spring Contest will be held on May 1st!

Participants in this competition will individually solve about 10 algorithm problems in 4 hours.

The competition will take place on Tuesday, May 1st, at N1 room 102, with the [Baekjoon Online Judge](https://acmicpc.net) platform. An online open contest (mirror) will be held simultaneously.

#### Schedule

* Registration closes on April 28th (Sun) at 23:59.
* The competition will be held on May 1st (Wed) at N1 102.
* Participants must arrive at the contest area by 6:00 pm.

```
Competition day schedule
18:00-18:30 | Competition guidance and environment setting
18:30-22:30 | Competition
22:30-23:00 | Commentary, awards ceremony, etc.
```

#### Prizes

300k KRW in cash prizes will be given in total.

There are special prizes for the 2 best-performing women and 2 best-performing foreign students, as well as special prizes given by other criteria. Winners of a main prize are ineligible for a special prize.

#### Rules

* Only KAIST students, including graduate students, exchange students, etc., can participate.
* You can only participate individually.
* Since no laptop will be provided, you must bring your own.
* Participants must arrive at the contest area by 6:00 pm.
* The available languages are expected to include C, C++, Java, Python, and Kotlin.
* Internet access is prohibited during the contest except for submitting solutions and accessing language reference sites.
* Using pre-written code or printouts from before the contest is not allowed.
* Anything not specified here will be decided at the organizers' discretion.

#### Contest Format

About 10 problems will be presented. Each problem consists of one or more subtasks, and each subtask is worth a fixed number of points. A subtask consists of several test data, and its points are awarded only if all of that data passes. The subtask points for each problem always sum to 100.

Each participant's ranking is determined as follows.

```
Penalty time = (time from contest start to the last submission that increased points)
Ranking = (# of participants with a higher total score) + (# of participants with the same score and a lower penalty time) + 1
```
