#### Outline

The 2023 KAIST RUN Spring Contest will be held on May 12th!

The competition will take place starting at 6:00 pm on Friday, May 12th, in KAIST N1 room 102.

#### Sponsorship

This contest is sponsored by [Naver D2](https://d2.naver.com), [Moloco](https://www.moloco.com/ko/), [Hancom Academy](https://www.hancomac.com/), and [Startlink](http://startlink.io).

#### Schedule

* The competition will be held on May 12th (Fri).
* Participants must arrive at N1 room 102 by 17:40.

```
Competition day schedule
17:40-18:00 | Competition guidance and environment setting
18:00-22:00 | Competition
22:00-22:20 | Seminar from sponsors (TBD)
22:20-      | Awards ceremony
```

#### Prizes

Prizes worth 1,500,000 KRW in total will be given.

* 1st place: iPad Air 64GB
* 2nd place: AirPods 3rd Generation
* 3rd place: Cherry MX Board 3.0 keyboard

#### Rules

* Only KAIST students, including graduate students, exchange students, etc., can participate.
* Each participant must bring their own laptop, laptop charger, and writing utensils.
* No internet or other reference materials may be consulted during the contest.

#### Scoring Environment

* The competition will be held through the "Contest" feature of [Baekjoon Online Judge](https://www.acmicpc.net/). Please familiarize yourself with the site beforehand.
* [Help for judge](https://www.acmicpc.net/help/judge)
* [Help for language](https://www.acmicpc.net/help/language)
