#### Outline

The 2023 KAIST 13th ICPC Mock Competition will be held on October 9th!

The competition will take place starting at noon on October 9th (Hangeul Day), in KAIST N1 room 102.

Registration is open until October 6th. We hope to see many KAIST students participate.

#### Schedule

* The competition will be held on October 9th (Mon).
* Participants must arrive at N1 room 102 by 11:40 am.

```
Competition day schedule
11:40-12:00 | Competition guidance and environment setting
12:00-17:00 | Competition
17:00-17:20 | Seminar from sponsors (TBD)
17:20-      | Awards ceremony
```

#### Rules

* Only KAIST students, including graduate students, exchange students, etc., can participate.
* Each participant must bring their own laptop, laptop charger, and writing utensils.
* This is a team contest with teams of up to 3 members.
* Each team may use up to 1 computer.
* The contest runs for 5 hours, with 12 problems.
* Each team may bring a single-sided printed team reference note of up to 20 pages, font size 5pt or larger. Organizers may ask to inspect team notes before the contest begins.

#### Scoring Environment

* The competition will be held through the "Contest" feature of [Baekjoon Online Judge](https://www.acmicpc.net/). Please familiarize yourself with the site beforehand.
* [Help for judge](https://www.acmicpc.net/help/judge)
* [Help for language](https://www.acmicpc.net/help/language)
